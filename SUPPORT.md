# OLWLG Beautifier Support

For help with OLWLG Beautifier, email
[olwlg.beautifier@gmail.com](mailto:olwlg.beautifier@gmail.com).

Please include:

- the OLWLG page where the issue occurred;
- what you expected to happen;
- what happened instead;
- your Chrome and OLWLG Beautifier versions; and
- a screenshot, if it does not contain information you prefer to keep private.

Do not send passwords, authentication cookies, or payment information.

endorsed by OLWLG or BoardGameGeek.
