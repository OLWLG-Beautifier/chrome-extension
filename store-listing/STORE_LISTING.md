## Detailed description

OLWLG Beautifier gives the OLWLG math-trade tools a clearer, more accessible
interface while preserving OLWLG's original controls and workflows.

Features include:

- A redesigned math-trade dashboard and navigation.
- Search, filters, sorting, and clearer item cards for trade catalogs.
- Progressively loaded BoardGameGeek cover images for catalog items.
- Focused and matrix want-list editors that remain synchronized with OLWLG's
  original form controls.
- Sticky matrix headings, drag-to-pan navigation, contextual tooltips, and
  review summaries.
- Clearer deadline, offer-phase, ended-trade, warning, participant, and
  read-only states.
- A compact toolbar popup that can enable or disable the enhanced interface.

The extension runs only on `bgg.activityclub.org/olwlg/`. It does not contain
advertising or analytics and does not send OLWLG page content to the developer.
See the privacy policy for details.

OLWLG Beautifier is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or
endorsed by OLWLG or BoardGameGeek.
